# Help Guide: Floor 6 Sign-in and Desktop Issues

Version: v 1.0
Date: 14/08/2026
Status: Draft
Source: Runbook-Floor6-Win11-Intune-Login-Access-Stabilization v1.0

We know some Floor 6 users may see slow sign-in, sign-in failure, or missing desktop shortcuts. Your files are not being deleted by this issue.

## What you can do now
1. Try one normal sign-in attempt and wait up to 2 minutes.
2. If it fails, take a screenshot of the message and note the time.
3. Check whether desktop shortcuts return after sign-in finishes.
4. Report your name, device name, and what happened to Service Desk.

## What not to do
- Do not keep retrying sign-in many times in a row.
- Do not change security or account settings yourself.

## If you need help
Contact Service Desk if sign-in still fails, takes too long, or shortcuts are still missing after one retry.

## Details to include in your help request
- Full name and work login name
- Device name (if known)
- Time issue started (local time)
- Exact error text or screenshot
- Whether issue was sign-in, slow desktop load, or missing shortcuts

We are actively monitoring this and will share scheduled updates.
