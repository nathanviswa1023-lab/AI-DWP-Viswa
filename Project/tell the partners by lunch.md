# Floor 6 Update for Partners (By Lunch)

Date: 14/08/2026  
Audience: Partners  
Prepared by: DWP Engineering and Service Desk

## What happened
This morning, Floor 6 (Legal) reported three issues at the same time:
- Some people could not sign in, or sign-in was much slower than normal.
- Some people saw slow performance after sign-in.
- One person reported that Copilot showed a client matter she believes she should not be able to see.

The sign-in and performance issues started after a new document app was rolled out to Floor 6 on Friday afternoon. We are treating the Copilot report as a potential access-control issue until checks are complete.

## What is being done now
- We have opened one coordinated incident and assigned dedicated leads for:
  - user access restoration,
  - floor performance stabilization,
  - access and security review.
- We have paused further spread of Friday's app change on Floor 6 while checks are underway.
- We are restoring access first for users who are blocked from urgent legal work.
- We are checking account access records and system activity records for the Copilot report.
- We are sending regular updates to Floor 6 and coordinating responses through a single incident channel.

## What is still open
- Whether all three symptoms have one common cause, or more than one cause.
- Whether the Copilot report reflects actual unauthorized access, or a false signal.
- Final timeline to full recovery for every affected user.

## Current risk position
- Business impact is high because Legal work is disrupted.
- Security status is still under active review.
- At this time, we are not confirming a data breach. We will confirm facts after access checks are complete.

## What partners should expect next
- Next formal update at: [insert time].
- Update content will include:
  - current affected user count,
  - confirmed findings,
  - immediate next actions,
  - revised timing outlook where available.

## Message for partner teams
We understand the urgency and reputational sensitivity of this incident. The team is focused on two priorities in parallel: restoring staff productivity and completing a careful access review. We will keep communication clear, factual, and regular, and we will not over-promise resolution timing before evidence is complete.
