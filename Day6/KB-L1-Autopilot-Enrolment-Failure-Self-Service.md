# Help Guide: New Device Setup (Autopilot) Not Completing

Version: v1.0
Date: 11/08/2026
Status: Draft

If your new device's automated setup (Autopilot) is not finishing, don't worry. Your files and data are safe — this happens before your account data is involved.

## What you can do now

1. Wait a few minutes, then restart the device once and let setup try again.
2. Make sure the device is connected to the internet (office Wi-Fi, wired network, or a trusted network) before setup continues.
3. If setup shows a message saying the device is "already enrolled" or similar, stop and do not keep retrying — this needs IT to fix a leftover record on the device.
4. If it's a device that was previously used by someone else or returned from storage, mention this when you contact support — it helps IT find the issue faster.

## What not to do

- Do not keep signing in and retrying setup many times in a row.
- Do not reset or reimage the device yourself.
- Do not attempt to manually remove work/school accounts from Settings unless IT asks you to.

## If you need help

Contact the Service Desk if setup still does not complete after these steps, or if you see any message mentioning "already enrolled in MDM."

## Details to include in your help request

Please include the details below so support can fix this faster:

- Your full name and work login name
- Device name and serial number (usually on a label on the device, or shown on the setup screen)
- Whether this device was used before by you or someone else, and roughly when
- Time the setup issue started (local time)
- The exact error message or code shown on screen, if any
- Your location (office/home) and connection type (office Wi-Fi, wired, home Wi-Fi)
- Which steps you tried from this guide

Thank you for your patience. Your data remains safe, and support will help complete your device setup as quickly as possible.
