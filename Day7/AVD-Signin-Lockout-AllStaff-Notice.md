# All-Staff Notice — AVD Sign-in & Account Lockout Issues

**Date:** 12/08/2026
**Audience:** All FinBridge staff
**Status:** Known issue — being actively investigated

---

Subject: We're aware of sign-in issues this morning — here's what to do

We're aware that a growing number of you are having trouble signing in to AVD this morning, including some being locked out of your account. This is a known issue and our IT team is actively investigating it — you don't need to report it individually if you're experiencing the same thing.

**What you can try in the meantime:**
1. Wait 15 minutes, then try signing in again.
2. If that doesn't work, restart your device fully and try once more.

If you're still unable to sign in after trying this, please contact the service desk and let them know your username and the time you first saw the issue.

We don't yet have a fixed resolution time, so please bear with us. We will send a further update by **12:00 today**, even if the issue is still being worked on.

Thank you for your patience — we know this is disruptive and we're on it.
