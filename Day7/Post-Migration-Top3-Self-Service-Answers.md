# Post-Migration Self-Service Answers — Top 3 Issues

---

## 1. Can't log in / keep getting locked out

If you can't sign in, or you've been locked out, try this first:

1. Wait 15 minutes, then try signing in again using the correct password — repeated wrong attempts too quickly is what usually triggers a lockout.
2. Restart your device once fully, then try signing in again.

If you're still locked out or can't sign in after trying this, contact the service desk. Please tell them:
- Your username
- The exact time you were locked out or first saw the problem
- How many times you've tried to sign in
- Whether this has happened before this week

---

## 2. My files seem to be missing

If files you expect to see are missing, try this first:

1. Check you're connected to the internet, then wait a few minutes — files can take time to reappear after the move.
2. Look in the recycle bin, and check the small sync icon near your file area for any warning symbol.

If your files still don't appear after this, contact the service desk straight away, especially if you need them today. Please tell them:
- Which file(s) or folder(s) are missing
- The last time you saw them
- Any deadline you're working to

---

## 3. Can't access a shared/team drive

If you can't open a shared drive you normally use, try this first:

1. Restart your device once, then try opening the drive again.
2. Check with a colleague on the same team to see if they have the same problem — this helps us know if it's just you or everyone.

If access still isn't working, contact the service desk. Please tell them:
- The exact name of the drive or folder you're trying to open
- Whether colleagues have the same issue
- What you're trying to do (e.g. a report or deadline this affects)
