## 2. Top 3 priorities to act on today

Ranking weighs **volume and impact together**. All three Blocker-severity themes are prioritised above every Friction/Minor/Positive theme regardless of comment count, since a Blocker means someone cannot work at all — a single Blocker comment outweighs many Friction or Minor ones. Among the three Blocker themes, volume and business-criticality are used to order them.

### 🥇 #1 — AVD Sign-in Failures & Account Lockouts (7 comments)

**Why it ranks #1:** This is both the highest-volume theme *and* Blocker severity — the worst combination possible. Affected users cannot work at all (no AVD session = no access to any systems), several report repeat lockouts (2nd/3rd time in a week), and at least one has been waiting 2+ hours with no callback. High volume confirms this isn't an isolated account issue but a systemic problem.

**Tell your manager:** Multiple staff are completely locked out of AVD today, some repeatedly and for hours with no callback — this is a full work-stoppage issue affecting the most people and needs urgent action, not just a password reset workaround.

### 🥈 #2 — OneDrive Files Missing / Sync Errors (4 comments)

**Why it ranks #2:** Second-highest volume among the Blocker themes, and every comment ties the missing files to a real, time-boxed deadline (an imminent meeting, a same-day Q1 report). Whether this is genuine data loss or a delayed sync/indexing issue, staff currently cannot retrieve files they need to do their job right now.

**Tell your manager:** Several staff can't access their OneDrive files against live deadlines today — we need to urgently confirm whether this is real data loss or just a sync delay, because right now they believe their work is missing.

### 🥉 #3 — Shared Drive Access Denied (S:/Finance) (3 comments)

**Why it ranks #3:** Lowest volume of the three Blocker themes, but it ranks in the top 3 anyway because of *impact over volume*: this is the finance team's shared drive, described as "completely inaccessible," directly blocking month-end reporting — a business-critical, time-sensitive process for a whole function, not just the 3 individuals who happened to comment.

**Tell your manager:** Only a handful of comments, but it's the finance team's shared drive completely down and blocking month-end reporting — small volume, high business risk, so it still belongs in today's top 3.

## 3. Themes considered but not in today's top 3

- **Floor 3 Printer Mapping Failure (6, Friction)** — higher volume than all three Blockers except AVD, but a workaround exists (walking to floor 2), so it doesn't stop work outright. Worth same-day attention but ranks below the Blockers.
- **VPN Connection Drops (4, Friction)** — disruptive during client calls but self-recovers; not a full work-stoppage.
- **Minor Cosmetic UI Changes (9, Minor)** and **Positive Feedback (9, Positive)** — highest raw volume of all themes, but low/no severity, so they do not outrank any Blocker or Friction theme under an impact-weighted ranking.
